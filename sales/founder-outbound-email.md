## Primary Version

Subject: Effort-based loyalty for athletic brands

Hi [Name],

I'm building PROOF, purpose-built effort-based loyalty infrastructure for
athletes and the brands that serve them.

Most promotional marketing starts when there's something to sell. PROOF starts
when the athlete has done the work, helping athletic brands build loyalty rooted
in identity and achievement.

For a brand, that means athletes can join a PROOF-powered program, connect a
trusted activity source, earn progress for eligible activity, unlock rewards,
and receive recognition when it is actually deserved: after verified activity,
milestones, goals, records, challenges, rewards, and consistent participation.

We're in friendly launch testing now with athlete-authorized activity
verification, and the system is built to add additional trusted effort sources
over time.

I would value your perspective as we shape the first brand conversations. Would
you be open to a 20-minute call?

Brian
